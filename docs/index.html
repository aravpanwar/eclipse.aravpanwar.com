The time is the longest totality anywhere on the path. Every eclipse here is computed from Besselian elements in NASA’s eclipse tables (Fred Espenak, NASA GSFC). Places are counted from GeoNames, which lists towns of 1,000 people or more, so a thinly settled path such as 2033’s has few. Only total eclipses are listed; annular and hybrid eclipses are not.
